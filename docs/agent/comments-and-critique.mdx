## Comment threads

Comments appear in the gutter and remain anchored to a text range. Open a thread to reply, send feedback, or inspect edits that belong to the discussion.

Use **Resolve** when the discussion is complete. Resolving a thread also drops its pending edits. Use **Reopen** when the discussion should continue.

When the anchor text is deleted, the thread becomes detached. Undoing the deletion can restore its anchor.

## Proposed fixes in comments

An agent message can include **Approve and propose edit**. Approving asks the agent to turn its explanation into a reviewable change.

Replying to a thread wakes the agent with the thread transcript. The agent can respond to the discussion and group later edits under the same thread.

## Critique passes

Open **Settings**, then **Critique pass**. Built in reviewers include PhD Advisor, Copy Editor, Skeptic, and Fresh Eyes.

<Frame>
  ![The built in critique passes in Settings](/images/critique-pass-menu.png)
</Frame>

A critique reads the active draft and can produce up to six focused findings. Findings arrive as comments, and a reviewer can also propose a fix. The reviewer name and mascot remain attached to its comments and edits.

Choose **New reviewer** to provide a name, mascot, color, and review prompt.
